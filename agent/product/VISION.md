# Product Vision

## Product
Kaizen — 30-Day Discipline Journal.

## Problem
People trying to rebuild personal discipline (sleep, focus, movement, restraint, spiritual grounding) lack a short, structured, self-contained program that combines a fixed daily ritual with honest reflection and real feedback on their own patterns. Generic habit trackers are either too open-ended (no arc, no end, no phases) or too gamified (streaks/badges) to support a serious, calm reset.

## Target users
A single individual, self-directed, running their own 30-day discipline reset. No coach, team, or account — one person, one device (or a few of their own devices via export/import), one active journey at a time.

## Desired outcome
The user completes (or honestly tracks an attempt at) a structured 30-day arc across four escalating phases, rates the same six indicators every day, reflects briefly, and — using nothing but their own logged data — sees where they are actually consistent, where they slip, and what that suggests for the next day and the next 30-day cycle.

## Product principles
- **Calm over gamified.** No badges, confetti, streak-shaming, or urgency dark patterns. Progress is shown plainly.
- **Small daily friction.** Logging a day (6 ratings + optional reflection) should take under a minute.
- **Data stays on the user's device.** No account, no backend, no transmission of journal content, in v1.
- **Insight only from real data.** Every number, trend, or insight shown must be derived from the user's own entries — never generic advice or fabricated content — and only shown once there is enough data to mean something.
- **Honest, not punitive.** Missed or low days are recorded factually (not hidden, not shamed); the point is visibility, not perfection.
- **Premium, minimal, Kaizen-inspired — not decorative.** The visual language favors restraint, whitespace, and quiet typography over literal Japanese imagery or cliché zen motifs.
