# Roadmap

## Now
- **M1 — Foundation & local data layer.** Project scaffold, versioned local persistence, app shell/navigation across the five views (empty states only, no business logic yet).

## Next
- **M2 — Onboarding & Today.** Start-date onboarding, daily indicator rating, day type, reflection, live adherence score, save/edit today.
- **M3 — 30-Day Plan.** All 30 days grouped into the 4 phases with correct status and drill-down.
- **M4 — Progress & insights.** Overall adherence, streaks, completed days, per-indicator averages, 30-day trend, indicator trends, data-only insights.

## Later
- **M5 — Weekly Review & History.** Per-week summary + weekly reflection; browse/edit any past day.
- **M6 — Data portability & visual system.** Export/import with confirmation; responsive polish; calm/minimal Kaizen visual system applied across all screens.
- **M7 — Release readiness.** Quality gates, accessibility and performance pass, release checklist.

See `agent/planning/MILESTONES.md` for entry/exit criteria per milestone.
